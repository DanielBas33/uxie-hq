# UXIE Solutions

> Purpose: stable context about UXIE Solutions.
> This document describes what UXIE is, what it offers, how it thinks, and the principles that should guide decisions about the business.
> Temporary priorities, current initiatives and unresolved topics belong in `current-state.md`.

---

## 1. What UXIE is

UXIE Solutions is a digital technology partner focused on helping businesses improve through:

- artificial intelligence
- automation
- UX/UI
- modern digital experiences

UXIE does not primarily sell technologies.

It identifies opportunities to improve how a business operates or how it presents and serves itself digitally, then designs and builds the technology required to achieve that improvement.

A useful internal principle is:

> Clients buy improvements to their business, not technologies.

---

## 2. What UXIE sells

UXIE's public-facing proposition is centered around two main areas.

### AI & Business Automation

UXIE helps businesses identify and implement opportunities to improve processes using artificial intelligence, automation and custom technology.

Typical outcomes may include:

- reducing repetitive manual work
- reducing operational time
- connecting disconnected tools and processes
- automatically processing information
- improving internal workflows
- introducing AI assistants or agents
- augmenting existing processes with AI
- creating new AI-enabled capabilities

The starting point should always be the business process or problem.

UXIE should not start from a specific tool and look for somewhere to apply it.

---

### Digital Experiences & UX/UI

UXIE helps businesses improve their digital presence and user experience.

This may include:

- modern websites
- UX/UI redesign
- improved digital journeys
- custom digital experiences
- content-driven websites
- specific interfaces or digital tools when required

The goal is not simply to deliver a website.

The goal is to create a digital experience aligned with:

- the quality of the business
- its brand
- its users
- its objectives

UXIE should avoid competing primarily in the low-cost template website market.

---

## 3. Technology is an enabler

UXIE has the technical capability to build the software required behind its solutions.

Depending on the problem, this may include:

- backend services
- APIs
- integrations
- databases
- cloud infrastructure
- authentication
- custom applications
- automation platforms
- AI services

These capabilities are important internally but are not the primary public-facing proposition.

For example, UXIE should prefer communicating:

> We automate a process that currently requires several hours of manual work.

rather than:

> We build FastAPI backends and n8n workflows.

And:

> We redesign and rebuild your digital experience.

rather than:

> We build Astro websites.

The implementation matters, but the outcome comes first.

---

## 4. Positioning

UXIE should position itself closer to a **digital technology partner** than to:

- a traditional web agency
- a software factory
- an n8n agency
- a low-code agency
- an AI wrapper agency

The intended relationship with a client is:

1. understand the business
2. identify the problem or opportunity
3. determine what should improve
4. design the appropriate solution
5. build the technology required
6. validate the result
7. evolve it when there is a clear reason to do so

UXIE should be capable of discussing both the business problem and the technical implementation.

---

## 5. Business-first thinking

Every solution should begin with:

- the current situation
- the business problem
- the user
- the desired outcome
- the constraints

Technology selection comes afterwards.

UXIE should avoid introducing technology simply because it is fashionable or technically interesting.

This applies especially to AI.

AI should only be introduced when it creates meaningful value.

---

## 6. Simplicity

UXIE prefers the simplest solution that correctly solves the problem.

Avoid unnecessary:

- infrastructure
- frameworks
- abstractions
- platforms
- services
- dependencies
- architectural complexity

Complexity must justify itself through a concrete requirement or benefit.

Simple does not mean low quality.

---

## 7. Quality

Solutions delivered by UXIE should aim for a high level of quality in the areas that matter to the project.

Depending on the solution, this may include:

- user experience
- visual quality
- performance
- maintainability
- accessibility
- security
- reliability
- responsive behavior
- observability
- documentation

UXIE should not sacrifice fundamental quality merely to deliver something quickly.

At the same time, quality should not become an excuse for unnecessary overengineering.

---

## 8. UX and technical quality belong together

UXIE should not treat design and engineering as completely separate concerns.

A good digital solution should combine:

- a clear user experience
- strong visual execution
- appropriate technical architecture
- good performance
- maintainable implementation

This is especially important for websites and customer-facing digital experiences.

---

## 9. Custom when it matters

UXIE favors solutions adapted to the actual needs of the business.

This does not mean everything must be built from scratch.

Existing platforms, SaaS products, automation tools and third-party services should be used when they provide the best solution.

Custom software should be introduced when it provides meaningful value that existing tools cannot provide cleanly.

---

## 10. Tool independence

UXIE should not define itself through specific technologies or vendors.

Tools may change over time.

Examples include:

- AI model providers
- automation platforms
- CMS platforms
- cloud providers
- frontend frameworks
- backend frameworks

The business problem and architecture should determine the technology, not the other way around.

---

## 11. Build for evolution

UXIE should not over-engineer solutions for hypothetical future requirements.

However, avoid unnecessarily locking solutions into choices that make future evolution difficult.

A good solution should solve today's problem while leaving reasonable paths for tomorrow.

---

## 12. Security and privacy

Security should be considered as part of the solution from the beginning.

Relevant considerations may include:

- authentication
- authorization
- secrets
- data exposure
- access control
- API security
- infrastructure exposure
- privacy
- client isolation

The depth required depends on the project, but security should never be treated purely as a final check.

---

## 13. How UXIE should work

A useful default process is:

`understand → identify opportunity → design → build → validate → improve`

UXIE should avoid jumping directly into implementation before understanding what is actually being improved.

When requirements are uncertain, prefer small validated steps over large speculative implementations.

---

## 14. AI philosophy

UXIE views AI as a practical business capability rather than an end in itself.

Relevant use cases may include:

- AI agents
- intelligent automation
- document processing
- extraction and classification
- assistants
- decision support
- content transformation
- workflow augmentation
- AI integrated into existing software

UXIE should avoid adding AI when conventional software or automation provides a simpler and more reliable solution.

---

## 15. Automation philosophy

Automation should eliminate friction, repetitive work and unnecessary manual coordination.

UXIE should first understand:

- what people are doing today
- why they are doing it
- where time is being lost
- where errors happen
- what systems are involved

Only then should the automation be designed.

Automation tools are implementation details, not the service itself.

---

## 16. Digital experience philosophy

A company's digital presence should reflect the quality of the underlying business.

UXIE should aim to create experiences that feel:

- modern
- deliberate
- clear
- fast
- trustworthy
- easy to use

Visual novelty should not come at the expense of usability or performance.

UX/UI decisions should have a purpose.

---

## 17. Knowledge and reuse

UXIE wants to progressively accumulate reusable knowledge.

This may include:

- standards
- playbooks
- technical patterns
- skills
- templates
- components
- infrastructure
- lessons learned
- decision records

Reuse should emerge from actual experience.

Prefer:

`real project → repeated pattern → validated pattern → reusable asset`

rather than designing large internal systems before they are needed.

---

## 18. Knowledge classification

Information about UXIE should be classified correctly.

### Context

Stable information that helps understand UXIE.

### Current state

Temporary information describing what UXIE is doing now.

### Decision

Something that has been explicitly accepted.

### Standard

A reusable rule about how UXIE normally works.

### Playbook

A repeatable operational process.

### Project context

Information that only applies to a specific project.

### Exploration

An idea that is being considered but has not yet been accepted.

An exploration must never automatically become a decision.

---

## 19. Accuracy and credibility

UXIE must never invent or exaggerate:

- clients
- projects
- case studies
- testimonials
- metrics
- results
- capabilities

Claims about UXIE should be based on:

- real experience
- actual delivered work
- explicitly documented capabilities
- verifiable results

Credibility should come from the quality of the work rather than inflated claims.

---

## 20. Commercial communication

Public communication should lead with outcomes and problems solved.

Prefer language around:

- improving a process
- saving time
- improving customer experience
- modernizing a digital presence
- eliminating repetitive work
- creating new capabilities with AI

Avoid leading with:

- programming languages
- frameworks
- infrastructure
- implementation details

Technical depth can be introduced when it helps establish credibility or when the audience requires it.

---

## 21. Long-term direction

UXIE aims to become increasingly effective through accumulated knowledge, reusable capabilities and AI-assisted operations.

Over time, the organization should become better at:

- identifying valuable opportunities
- designing solutions
- delivering them efficiently
- reusing proven patterns
- making consistent decisions
- learning from previous projects

The objective is not to accumulate documentation for its own sake.

The objective is to create a system in which previous work improves future work.

---

## 22. Core principle

When evaluating any opportunity, UXIE should ask:

> What meaningful improvement can we create for this business?

Only after answering that question should UXIE determine which combination of:

- AI
- automation
- UX/UI
- software
- integrations
- infrastructure

is required to make it real.
