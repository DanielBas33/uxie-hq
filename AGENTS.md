# UXIE HQ

This repository is the operating knowledge base for UXIE Solutions.

Use these sources as needed:

- `docs/context/uxie.md` — stable UXIE context and positioning.
- `docs/context/daniel.md` — Daniel's capabilities and working style.
- `docs/context/current-state.md` — current priorities and open topics.
- `docs/design-system/uxie.md` — canonical UXIE visual direction and its decision status.
- `docs/decisions/` — accepted decisions.
- `docs/playbooks/` — repeatable processes.

Rules:

- Do not invent UXIE facts, clients, pricing, metrics or capabilities.
- Distinguish facts, decisions and ideas under exploration.
- Current explicit decisions override older context.
- Think business-first: UXIE sells improvements, not technologies.
- Prefer simple, practical solutions over unnecessary complexity.
- Do not promote an idea into canonical documentation unless Daniel explicitly accepts it.
- For UXIE visual, interface, website, document or presentation work, consult
  `docs/design-system/uxie.md`. Follow confirmed guidance, keep provisional guidance
  easy to revise, and do not treat open questions as decided.
